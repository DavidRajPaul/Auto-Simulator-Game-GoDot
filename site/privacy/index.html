Privacy Policy

Namma Auto: Chennai Rickshaw Simulator · Android · Last updated 27 November 2026

Short version: the game has no accounts and never asks for your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or location. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID, and anonymous crash reports help us fix bugs.

This policy explains what information the Android game Namma Auto: Chennai Rickshaw Simulator (package com.davidrajpaul.nammaauto, “the game”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by David Paul, an independent developer in Chennai, India (“we”, “us”).

1. Information we do not collect

The game has no sign-in or user accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise or approximate location. We do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (money, autos, upgrades, levels, achievements, settings and the in-game day) is saved in an encrypted file inside the game's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward, occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (never while you drive), and banner ads on some menu screens. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • IP address (which can indicate your general region), and ad interactions such as views and taps.

This information is collected and used by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load, and you can choose non-personalised ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Crash and error reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, it may send a technical report so we can fix it: the error and code location, the game version, device model, Android version, graphics setting, language, the screen you were on and basic game state (for example which auto you were driving). Reports contain no name, contact details, account or advertising ID, and we do not use them for advertising. They are processed by an error-tracking service built on the open-source GlitchTip / Sentry software and kept only as long as needed to fix problems (at most 90 days).

5. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the game schedules them on your phone. They are local notifications: no server or push service is involved, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android settings at any time.

8. Children

The game is a general-audience driving game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security and retention

Saves are encrypted on your device. Crash reports are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and deleted after at most 90 days. Information processed by Google is retained under Google's policies.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off personalised ads or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.
  • Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's data practices change (for example, if in-app purchases or cloud saves are added) and change the “Last updated” date above.
