# Chennai architecture audit — work in progress

Playable bounds: south 13.028, west 80.230, north 13.070, east 80.288. Origin 13.049, 80.259. Preserve the map bounds.

The normal building atlas is generated by tools/art/make_facade_atlas.py. It is original painted art, not a collection of photographs of individual Chennai buildings. OSM footprints supply the shapes. Landmark fidelity is a separate gap: LIC and Vivekananda House were plain boxes, Napier Bridge is represented by two coloured boxes, and Gemini Flyover has a simplified deck and columns.

## Initial implementation

LIC now has a repeated window grid, projecting floor bands and roof cap. Vivekananda House has a pink curved frontage, cream cornices and recessed openings. Both use the existing reserved locations, one mesh/material each and existing ground-snap/collision integration. These are architectural approximations, not measured replicas or exact photographic textures. Visual review, correct façade orientation and engine/performance validation are still required. Do not mark the broader semi-realism work complete from these two changes.

## Remaining work

- Audit the rendered city in daylight, dusk and rain, using actual game captures.
- Verify landmark positions against current OSM data; the existing tower pipeline offsets the LIC marker from its source coordinates.
- Survey Santhome, Parthasarathy Temple, Chepauk Palace, University Senate House, Presidency College, Valluvar Kottam and the Marina memorials against the precise bounding rectangle and existing footprints before adding them.
- Rebuild Napier Bridge arches and Gemini junction geometry with road clearance checks.
- Source commercially reusable, attributed façade photos or user-owned photos; rectify perspective, remove baked shadows/foreground objects, atlas and mipmap them. A single oblique photo on a cube will distort doors/windows and repeat lighting incorrectly.
- Upgrade nearby ordinary street fronts, pavement edges and colour/roughness variation so landmarks belong to the same visual world.
- Review screenshots before/after and measure on the budget Android target. CI draw-call success alone does not establish visual realism or phone FPS.

## APK test pass: implemented coverage

- Mylapore: Kapaleeshwarar entrance piers and repeated sculptural relief on the gopuram tiers.
- Marina: Vivekananda House curved frontage and Napier Bridge repeated white arches; existing imported lighthouse remains.
- Anna Salai: LIC window grid and projecting floor bands.
- Santhome: pale church walls, west entrance tower, spire and cross on the existing OSM cathedral footprint.
- University waterfront: Senate House red walls and corner pavilions.
- Chepauk: both palace footprints receive red walls, cornices and capped corner towers.

The last four footprint treatments are baked into each existing chunk building mesh with no extra draw calls. Existing footprint collision and pedestrian exclusion remain in effect. The matcher requires both OSM-derived seed and a nearby footprint centroid, and a phase 9 content test verifies all four matches. An OSM rebuild that moves/removes these footprints must revisit the anchors.

Test route: Mylapore temple → Santhome → Lighthouse → Vivekananda House → Chepauk → Senate House → Napier Bridge → LIC. Use the map/custom destination controls. Inspect daylight and dusk; check driving clearance and that no tower intersects its neighbours. The new geometry is an architectural interpretation, not a measured reproduction. Original painted atlas textures remain; no photographic textures or new third-party images have been shipped. Parthasarathy and Valluvar Kottam are still pending verified placement and dedicated models.
