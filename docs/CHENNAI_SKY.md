# Chennai sky through the day

Research behind `Atmosphere.SKY_KEYS` and `shaders/chennai_sky.gdshader`.

## What the sources say

* **Vivid sunsets need high and mid clouds.** Cirrus and altocumulus show the strongest reds and oranges because they catch sunlight above the dirty lower air. Low cloud rarely glows, and low haze dulls colour ([NOAA, The Science of Sunsets](https://spc.noaa.gov/publications/corfidi/sunset.html)).
* **Colour differs across the sky.** The horizon reddens while the sky overhead and away from the sun stays blue (same source).
* **Chennai sunsets:** orange and red streaks and crepuscular "Buddha rays" are reported in the pre-monsoon season, linked to moisture left by inland thunderstorms ([inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/come-sunset-the-sky-is-a-riot-of-colour)).
* **Chennai mornings:** a local forecaster describes partly cloudy skies with "beautiful cumulus clouds" and good sunrise photography at the beach ([Kea Weather](https://kwschennai.com/blog/pleasant-dry-weather-to-continue/?amp=1)).
* **Sun times:** in June, sunrise is about 5:41 and sunset 6:32 to 6:39 PM ([sunrise-sunset.net](https://www.sunrise-sunset.net/india/chennai)). The game keeps 6:00 and 6:30 for the whole year.
* **Northeast monsoon:** rain mostly comes from low-pressure systems, with amorphous and banded cloud ([MAUSAM](https://mausamjournal.imd.gov.in/index.php/MAUSAM/article/view/1960)).

The hex colours are art direction from these descriptions, not measurements.

## The day in the game

| Time | Look |
|---|---|
| 5:30 | First light over the Bay of Bengal: peach horizon, deep blue above, first stars fading |
| 6:00 | Sunrise from the sea: warm horizon, pink opposite |
| 8:00 to noon | Deep blue overhead, pale near the horizon, bright white cumulus |
| 4:00 PM | Blue with a warmer horizon toward the sun |
| 5:30 PM | Golden light, clouds turning cream |
| 6:15 PM | Orange horizon, lilac sky, cirrus turning gold, rays behind cloud |
| 6:30 PM | Peak: red-orange horizon, red cirrus, purple shadows, blue sky opposite |
| 6:45 PM | Afterglow: magenta cirrus, sun below the horizon |
| 7:00 PM | Violet dusk, a red line on the horizon, first stars |
| 7:25 PM on | Deep blue night, stars and moon, with a faint city glow |

Each day picks one of six skies, from clear blue to full of cirrus, so sunsets vary.
