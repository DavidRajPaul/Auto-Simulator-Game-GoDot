loop seamlessly). **Licences are NOT yet verified** for these sounds: the Freesound pages
could not be reached from the build environment. Check each licence on its Freesound page and fill in
the table before a release. Attribution-licensed sounds need their author credited in the game's
credits screen; CC0 ones do not.
